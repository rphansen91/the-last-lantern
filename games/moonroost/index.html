MOONROOST

Every grove keeps exactly one owl. Owls keep to themselves: never two in a row or a column, and never wing to wing, not even at a corner.

Roosting rules

One owl per grove

  • Each colored grove needs exactly one owl.
  • Every row and every column holds exactly one owl.
  • Owls never touch, not side by side and not at a corner.
  • Tap a branch to cross it out, or drag to cross out a whole line. Tap a cross to clear it.
  • Double-tap to land an owl. A wrong perch costs a moon, and you have three per grove. Every grove can be solved without guessing, so Listen if you get stuck.
