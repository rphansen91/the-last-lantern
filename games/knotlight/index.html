Night Arcade

KNOTLIGHT

Glowing hoops hang hitched together in the dark. Twist each one until its gap slips the clip that holds it, and every freed ring rises into the sky as a star.

How to play

  • Drag a ring round to turn it. Its opening travels with it.
  • Clips are the little sleeves where two pieces meet. A clip is the colour of the piece it belongs to, and it hooks the other piece. Turn the hooked ring until its gap reaches the clip and the clip slips off.
  • A piece that still owns a clip is pinned and won't budge. Free the ring it hooks first.
  • A piece with no clips left flies up and becomes a star. Clear the board.
  • Brass studs can't pass a clip or an iron peg, so turn the other way. Closed rings have no gap, so unhook everything around them. Bars slide off along their arrows once they're free.
  • Embers burn down one per move. Free that ring before it hits zero.
  • Hint shows a good move. Snip cuts any one clip, once per board. Undo is unlimited.

Every board can be cleared without Snip or Hint.
