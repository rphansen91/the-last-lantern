BAYSHIFT

The night ferry leaves at the whistle. Drag every crate out through the bay door of its color — if it fits — before the clock runs dry.

Dock rules

How it plays

  • Drag a crate anywhere it can slide. It moves along the deck and stops at walls, bollards and other crates.
  • Push it into a bay door of its color. Every row or column the crate covers must fit inside the door — wide loads need wide doors.
  • Frosted crates thaw after the number of loads shown. Rail crates slide one way only. Wrapped crates shed one color per bay. Shutters roll up after enough loads of their color.
  • Beat the clock. Undo is free. One Break (freeze 10s) and one Lift (remove a crate) per bay.
